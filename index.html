What I'm seeing

You don't need more content. You need a container.

  • There's no doorway in yet

    Your nutrition practice serves everyone, men and women of every age. Your menopause work serves women in transition. When someone lands on your world, they can't tell which door is theirs. The doorway we landed on: women in perimenopause and menopause who want to wake up excited about life again. Nutrition, digestion, HRT, purpose, all of it lives inside once she's in.

  • Your best free stuff is leaking out the side

    You give away a lot in your newsletter and blog. Yogurt and probiotics for longevity. Building and keeping muscle mass. Great content, and it disappears into inboxes and spam folders. Nobody gets to experience you, so nobody moves closer.

  • The membership has no specific reason to join

    Women don't join a group because it offers "a bit of everything." They join because there's one thing inside they want right now. A course, a challenge, a process. That's the missing piece behind the $79 crickets, not your price and not your value.

  • One-on-one has a ceiling

    Fifteen clients a week across three days is a beautiful life. It's also a cap on income, and every client who finishes means going back out to replace them. Recurring community income is what lets you take a month off and still get paid.
